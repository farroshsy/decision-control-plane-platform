08 / Policy Execution

The Policy Interpreter turns facts into governed action

Input Contract
  • Typed facts & evidence snapshot
  • Context & state
  • Policy bundle
  • Evaluation clock
Output Contract
  • Disposition & obligations
  • Routes & missing-data behavior
  • Fired rules & counterevidence
  • Explanation & trace hash
Hard Constraints First
Hard constraints run before soft scoring or optimization. Law and eligibility cannot be reduced to statistical ranking.
Explicit States
true, false, unknown, and conflicted are explicit states. Missing data must not silently become safe.
Lifecycle & Testing
Policies are effective-dated, immutable after activation, and tested in shadow mode before deployment.
Regulatory Alignment: OFAC's framework similarly emphasizes risk assessment, internal controls, testing, audit, recordkeeping, and rapid response to changing sanctions data.
