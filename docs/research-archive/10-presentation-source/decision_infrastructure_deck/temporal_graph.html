05 / Temporal Graph

High-frequency screening is a temporal graph problem

Identity & Relationships
Party identity is not enough: include accounts, beneficial owners, intermediaries, affiliates, wallets, and jurisdictions.
Lists & Behavior
Screen both lists and behavior: transaction monitoring is a subset of broader suspicious-activity monitoring.
Dual Clocks
Use event time and processing time explicitly to handle late, out-of-order, or retrospective data.
Match States
Treat candidate match, confirmed match, anomaly, and legal conclusion as distinctly different states.
