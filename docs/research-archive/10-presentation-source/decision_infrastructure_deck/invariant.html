02 / Foundational principle

The invariant is a replayable decision event

“Every consequential decision is a replayable, evidence-bound event over a versioned relationship state.”Not a score. Not a dashboard. A durable decision artifact.
Evidence
Time-stamped, scoped, sourced, uncertainty-aware.
Relationships
Exposure and dependency are first-class.
Constraints
Policy is separated from prediction.
Lifecycle
Action, approval, monitoring, and outcome stay linked.
