01 / Pattern recognition

The same decision recurs under different names

Manufacturing
Approve, allocate to, monitor, or replace a supplier?
Continuity · quality · capacity · recovery time
Financial markets
Trade with, limit, collateralize, or close out a counterparty?
Bilateral exposure · concentration · settlement
Public procurement
Award, reject, condition, or monitor a contractor?
Public value · delivery · integrity · eligibility
Clinical research
Select, monitor, remediate, or close a site?
Safety · data quality · enrollment · timelines
Core insight: the object is not an entity score; it is a contextual relationship decision.
