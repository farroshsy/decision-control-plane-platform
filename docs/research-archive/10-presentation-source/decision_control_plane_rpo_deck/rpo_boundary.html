01 / RPO admission

Why RPO is a decision boundary

Freshness changes what a transaction screening system is allowed to do.
State 01 · Within budget
Fresh
Ownership is valid, policy and state epochs match, trace is durable, and remote acknowledgement remains within the tenant RPO.
State 02 · Budget pressured
Degraded
The system can continue bounded work, but must expose uncertainty. Risky decisions are held, retried, or routed for review.
State 03 · Budget breached
Unsafe
Allow-like side effects stop. The outcome becomes hold, reject, retry, or fence until authority and durability are re-established.
The system must never turn stale state into a silent allow.
