06 / The Executive Decision

Fund the bounded pilot.
Scale only on evidence.

The protocol is the stable kernel. Storage, replication technology, policy models, and domain adapters remain replaceable.

1. Fund bounded pilot

Deploy the production pilot with the RPO admission control and fencing protocol enabled from day one.

2. Enforce invariants

Require zero split-brain writes, zero duplicate actions, and exact replay equivalence before accepting the system.

3. Scale on evidence

Expand to new domains and regions only after the customer-specific deployment passes signed RTO/RPO targets.
