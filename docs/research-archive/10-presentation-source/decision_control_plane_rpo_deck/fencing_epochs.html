03 / Fencing and Epochs

Fencing is stronger than routing

DNS and load balancers can stop new traffic, but they cannot prove an old writer has stopped. Fencing is an authorization fact enforced at write time.
State 01
Epoch 1: Region A
Region A holds the active lease. Writes are accepted because the request epoch matches the current ownership epoch.
State 02
Quorum Fence
A partition occurs. Quorum authority revokes Epoch 1. Region A becomes a stale writer and its writes are explicitly rejected.
State 03
Epoch 2: Region B
Region B acquires Epoch 2 and becomes the sole writer. Split-brain is prevented because Region A cannot write under Epoch 1.
