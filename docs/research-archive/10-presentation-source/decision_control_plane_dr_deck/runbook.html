04 / Operational runbook

High-consequence operations require explicit authority

The runbook defines who approves, who executes, and what evidence is required for the four most critical platform operations.
Tenant Migration
Move a tenant between regions or isolation tiers. Requires shadow execution, replay comparison, dual-write reconciliation, and a tested rollback window.
Owner: Tenant Owner & Platform Lead
Key Rotation
Rotate cryptographic keys without losing history. Requires rewrapping data in bounded batches, verifying reads, and retaining old keys for legal holds.
Owner: Security Owner
Partition Response
Handle regional impairment or network splits. Requires fencing the stale epoch, holding decisions after RPO breach, and reconciling actions upon healing.
Owner: Incident Commander & Compliance
Disaster Recovery Drills
Prove resilience claims with evidence. Includes monthly traffic evacuation, quarterly regional rebuilds, and semiannual split-brain game days.
Owner: Platform Lead & Auditor
