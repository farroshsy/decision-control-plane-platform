07 / The Executive Decision

Fund proof now.
Scale only on measured evidence.

The Decision Control Plane earns its platform status by proving that one decision can cross domains without losing meaning, governance, or resilience.

1. Prove the kernel

Deploy the AML lighthouse slice and validate the canonical event model, active-active failover, and RPO controls.

2. Measure the economics

Use pilot telemetry to confirm direct savings and operational value before committing to migration.

3. Protect the abstraction

Keep the kernel stable and use the Domain Adapter SDK so future domains remain pluggable.
