05 / Validation Gates

Production gates before scale

The platform must prove its resilience and correctness through measured evidence before expanding beyond the first vertical slice.
01
Split-brain prevention
Zero dual-authority writes during partition drills. Stale regions must be successfully fenced.
02
Action idempotency
Zero duplicate side effects across failover boundaries. Retries must reconcile safely.
03
RPO admission control
High-risk decisions must be held or rejected when replication lag exceeds the defined budget.
04
Replay equivalence
≥99.99% exact reproduction of pinned historical decisions using the immutable event ledger.
05
Tenant isolation
Zero cross-tenant read or write findings during negative testing of the authorization context.
06
Recovery time (RTO)
Takeover, projection rebuilds, and controlled failback executed within agreed operational windows.
