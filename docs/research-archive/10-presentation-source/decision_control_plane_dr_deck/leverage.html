06 / Leverage Plan

How to leverage the work

Convert the research into a repeatable enterprise decision product through a phased land-and-expand strategy.
Step 01
90-Day Lighthouse
Prove the kernel on one AML vertical slice. Run in shadow mode, compare outputs, and measure the true cost per decision.
Step 02
Reusable Kernel
Freeze the canonical event, decision episode, and replay contracts. Establish the core infrastructure that remains stable across domains.
Step 03
Adapter SDK & Assurance
Package the gRPC contracts, compliance/XAI report generators, and DR runbooks to make the architecture enterprise-deployable.
Step 04
Second-Domain Expansion
Expand to supplier or procurement risk only after the first slice proves economic value and adapter isolation.
