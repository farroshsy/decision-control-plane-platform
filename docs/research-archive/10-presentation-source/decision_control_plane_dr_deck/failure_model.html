01 / Failure model

What happens when regions disagree?

The hardest failure is not downtime. It is allowing two regions to believe they are authoritative at the same time.
01 — DUAL AUTHORITY

Split brain

Two regions accept writes for the same tenant or decision aggregate. The system can no longer prove which policy state or ownership epoch was authoritative.
02 — SIDE EFFECTS

Duplicate action

A retry crosses the failover boundary and issues a second hold, release, block, or report. A technical retry becomes a real-world duplicate.
03 — PROOF LOSS

Unverifiable history

Events, evidence, or action outcomes are missing or mixed across versions. The decision may have been correct, but the organization cannot reconstruct it.
Safe design principle: serve locally, fence globally, reconcile explicitly.
