06 / Validation gates

Hard gates before authority

Authority changes only after these hard gates pass. We scale on evidence, not on architectural theory.
01. Correctness
No unexplained critical decision divergence in the approved replay corpus.
02. Determinism
≥99.99% exact replay equivalence for pinned cases; all exceptions classified.
03. Fencing
Zero stale-epoch writes accepted in partition drills.
04. Actions
Zero duplicate side effects; all retries reconcile to one logical command.
05. RPO
Allow-like actions held or rejected when the tenant RPO budget is exceeded.
06. Isolation
Zero cross-tenant read or write findings during negative testing.
07. Operations
A non-authoring operator can execute the runbook successfully and meet signed RTO targets.
