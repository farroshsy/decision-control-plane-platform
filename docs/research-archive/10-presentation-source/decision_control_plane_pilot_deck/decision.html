08 / The Executive Decision

Fund the bounded pilot.
Scale only on evidence.

The product is not only a faster decision engine; it is a safer, replayable, and operable way to change decision authority.

1. Fund bounded pilot

Deploy the production pilot on one transaction rail and one tenant boundary, keeping the incumbent available.

2. Protect the kernel

Keep the core stable. Use the Domain Adapter SDK so future domains remain pluggable without forking the architecture.

3. Expand on evidence

Expand to new domains and regions only after the customer-specific deployment passes signed RTO/RPO targets and hard gates.
