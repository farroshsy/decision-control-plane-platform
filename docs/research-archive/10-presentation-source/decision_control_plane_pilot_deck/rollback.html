Canary, rollback, and day two

Cutover is reversible, but rollback is a controlled ownership transition, not a simple DNS switch. Operations require a strict rhythm.
The Rollback Protocol
01
Stop new writes through the admission gate for the affected cohort.
02
Reconcile actions to ensure no in-flight commands are left ambiguous.
03
Fence the epoch to revoke the Decision Control Plane's write authority.
04
Advance incumbent ownership epoch and route traffic back to the legacy engine.
05
Preserve evidence and events for post-incident replay and divergence classification.
Ongoing Operating Cadence
Daily
Review latency tails, RPO lag, audit backlog, action unknowns, and policy drift.
Weekly
Reconcile sampled decisions, refresh evidence-quality metrics, and review support access.
Monthly
Run partition, traffic evacuation, action-rail failure, and key-readiness drills.
Quarterly
Run regional rebuild, split-brain game day, TCO refresh, and stakeholder approval review.
