09 / Stress Test

The stress test changes the architecture

Simulation Results
  • Policy Interpreter capacity: ~16,000 tx/s
  • Audit Commit capacity: ~10,667 tx/s
  • At 10,000 tx/s, audit commit reaches 93.9% utilization (dangerously close to saturation).
  • At 12,500 tx/s, the audit queue becomes unstable (117.1% util), while the interpreter is only at 78.3%.
Architectural Response
  • Durable audit logging cannot be an unbounded synchronous database write.
  • Separate the compact synchronous trace from asynchronous rich-record persistence.
  • Audit persistence must be a separately scalable subsystem.
  • Backpressure must be a policy-visible state, never a silent bypass.
Conclusion Scaling the interpreter alone will not increase end-to-end throughput once audit capacity is exhausted.
