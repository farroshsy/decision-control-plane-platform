06 / The Executive Takeaway

Observable by design.
Measure semantic safety.

We do not infer health from infrastructure metrics like CPU or network throughput. We measure the actual safety of decisions, authority, and evidence.

1. Semantic Safety

Monitor idempotency, fencing rejections, RPO admission, and audit durability to guarantee that the system is operating within its safety envelope.

2. Explicit Degradation

Ensure that when the system degrades, it fails safely and visibly, turning uncertainty into typed outcomes rather than silent errors.

3. Operational Readiness

Equip operators with the exact telemetry needed to answer "is it safe to allow?" in real time during a high-frequency incident.
