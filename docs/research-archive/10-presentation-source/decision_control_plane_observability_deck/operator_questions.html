01 / Operational Control

The five critical operator questions

When a high-frequency engine degrades, operators do not have time to write SQL queries. They need instant answers to five safety questions.
01
Can this tenant safely issue a decision right now?
Is the system free of split-brain accepts, duplicate actions, and critical alerts?
02
Is replication inside the RPO budget?
Are we operating within the freshness limit, or should allow-like actions be held?
03
Are stale writers being fenced?
Is a partitioned region safely rejecting writes, or is it still accepting unsafe traffic?
04
Who owns the current epoch?
Which region holds the authoritative lease, and is the epoch synchronized?
05
Is the audit trail durable?
Can every decision and action still be reconstructed from the immutable ledger?
