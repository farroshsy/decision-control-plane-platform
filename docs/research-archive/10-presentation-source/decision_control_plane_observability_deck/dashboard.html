The operator dashboard layout

The Grafana dashboard is organized into six rows, prioritizing safety over throughput. A red state means "unsafe to allow," not just "unavailable."
Row 01
Executive Health
Green only when there are zero split-brain accepts, zero duplicate actions, and valid ownership. Shows minimum RPO headroom.
Row 02
Replication & Ownership
Time-series for lag vs. hard RPO budget, current owner region and epoch, and tenant-by-region lag heatmap.
Row 03
Admission Latency
p50/p95/p99 histograms decomposed into policy evaluation, evidence lookup, RPO gate, and decision append time.
Row 04
Fencing & Degraded
Fencing rejections by reason, RPO holds over time, stale epoch request rates, and split-brain accepts (always critical).
Row 05
Actions & Audit
Action command rate, acknowledgement p99, unknown outcomes, duplicate suppression, and audit append latency.
Row 06
Drill-down Links
Tenant-scoped links to decision timelines, state manifests, action reconciliation queues, and XAI reports.
