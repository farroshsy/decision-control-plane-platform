# BPMN graph asset review

## AML screening flow

The AML screening graph is a wide 3120×648 flowchart showing transaction proposal, canonicalization, state-manifest validation, bounded candidate generation, DMN/policy decision, hold/allow paths, decision episode, action execution, action acknowledgement, outcomes, learning/calibration, and material reassessment.

## Decision-case-event graph

The decision-case-event graph is a 3120×1652 layered view. The top layer contains BPMN, DMN, CMMN, statechart/Petri net, and CEP. The central runtime layer contains process/case instance, activity instance, decision episode, evidence, policy evaluation, action/obligation, and outcome/reassessment. The evidence ledger contains immutable event, replay manifest, and audit/access record. This graph is useful for explaining that the Decision Control Plane is a semantic and evidentiary substrate across model families rather than a replacement for them.

## Presentation usage

The AML flow is suitable for a full-width process slide. The relationship graph is dense and should be used either as a full-width technical appendix slide or cropped into a presentation callout. Both images are rendered successfully and are suitable for delivery.
