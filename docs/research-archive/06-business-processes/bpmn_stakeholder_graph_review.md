# Stakeholder BPMN graph review

## Target-market stakeholder journey

The rendered graph is 3120×1800 and organizes four conceptual areas: target market and buying center, customer enterprise roles, people and organizations of interest, and Decision Control Plane responsibilities. It shows the commercial path from market trigger through economic buyer, champion, procurement, and purchase decision, alongside implementation, production use, analyst review, compliance, engineering, security/privacy, audit/model risk, regulator, and business-product roles.

## People-of-interest screening

The rendered graph is 3120×1072 and shows originator/applicant, beneficiary/recipient, beneficial owner/controller, intermediary/counterparty, transaction/account/device event, and KYC/customer master entering a Domain Adapter. The path continues through normalized roles, relationship resolution, bounded context, candidate facts, state manifest, policy evaluation, decision episode, action/obligation, human stakeholders, case/reporting, findings, reassessment, and calibration.

## Usage

The first graph is suited to enterprise buying-center and stakeholder workshops. The second graph is suited to AML operating-model design and clarifies that people of interest are modeled as subject roles and evidence-bearing relationships, not as an undifferentiated risk score.
